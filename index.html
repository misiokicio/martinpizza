The Secret is in the Dough

We don't believe in shortcuts. Every single ball of dough we serve undergoes a meticulous 24 to 48-hour fermentation process.

By allowing the dough to rest and develop slowly, we create a crust that is incredibly light, airy, and easy to digest—featuring the classic charred 'leopard spotting' that only comes from high-quality hydration and 500°C heat.

Combined with San Marzano tomatoes and creamy Fior di Latte, it's the authentic taste of Naples, right here in Scotland.

Dietary & Vegan Requirements

We believe absolutely nobody should have to miss out on exceptional pizza at your event. We proudly offer tailored options to ensure every single one of your guests is safely and deliciously catered for.

🌿 100% Vegan Friendly

Any pizza on our sample menu can be customized using our premium, creamy vegan cheese substitute. It melts beautifully in our 500°C ovens, ensuring your vegan and dairy-intolerant guests get the exact same authentic Neapolitan style profile.

🌾 Gluten Intolerances

We supply high-quality alternate pizza crust alternatives specifically crafted for guests with gluten intolerances. Please Note: Because our mobile trailer operates with traditional wheat flour continuously airborne inside the workspace, we cannot guarantee a 100% completely trace-free environment required for severe Coeliac disease.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas across Scotland do you cover for pizza catering?
We are proudly based in Bathgate, West Lothian, and routinely cater events across West Lothian, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Falkirk, Stirling, and the Central Belt. Depending on the size of your booking, we can also provide nationwide catering across Scotland.
How much space does your mobile pizza trailer require?
Our custom professional pizza trailer requires flat, accessible ground surface area to set up safely and park. When you send over your event location details, we verify the vehicle positioning to make sure logistics roll perfectly on the day.
Do you cater for vegan guests and food allergies?
Yes! We stock premium vegan melting cheeses for dairy-free or plant-based requirements. We also provide specialty alternative bases for guests with gluten intolerances. However, because our trailer environment features open flour handling, it is not suitable for severe Coeliac profiles.
What happens if the Scottish weather turns bad?
Don't worry! Our catering trailer is a heavy-duty, completely self-contained, weather-protected mobile kitchen. We operate perfectly in the rain and wind, keeping our stone ovens blazing at 500°C so your event timeline goes completely undisturbed.
How many guests can you cater for?
We comfortably cater for events from 10 up to 200 guests. Whether it's an intimate garden party or a large wedding reception, our setup scales to suit your numbers. Just let us know your expected guest count when you get in touch.
